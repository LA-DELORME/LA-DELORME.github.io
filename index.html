01 / About

Experience across disciplines. A commitment to people.

I bring together entrepreneurial experience, healthcare knowledge, and a practical approach to helping organizations and communities thrive.

My professional journey spans business ownership, executive scheduling and travel coordination, hospitality, nonprofit development, Traditional East Asian Medicines, clinical healthcare, integrative skincare, international sport, community health, education, and conflict resolution.

That experience strengthened my ability to coordinate services, train staff, manage competing priorities, build lasting relationships, and respond thoughtfully to the needs of clients.

Across clinical, educational, nonprofit, and international settings, I have learned to connect people, systems, and ideas through careful observation, adaptability, practical judgment, and thoughtful service.

I am interested in opportunities involving operations, program coordination, community engagement, healthcare, education, and collaborative service delivery. I value technical competence alongside compassion, integrity, curiosity, and respect for the people behind every process.

04 / Areas of Practice

Integrative Esthetics

Skin Health • Whole-Person Care • Healthy Aging

My approach to integrative esthetics brings together contemporary skincare practices and traditional therapeutic perspectives, recognizing the relationship between skin health, lifestyle, environment, and overall well-being. With training in esthetics, Korean cosmetic acupuncture, and microneedling, I am particularly interested in approaches that support skin vitality, healthy aging, and individualized care. I value treatments that honor both the science of skin health and the experience of the person receiving care.

Acupuncture & Traditional East Asian Medicines

Root & Branch • Traditional Diagnosis • Individualized Treatment

My clinical approach is grounded in traditional diagnostic principles, with particular attention to the relationship between root and branch, understanding both the underlying patterns of disharmony and their manifestations. My practice incorporates acupuncture, Tui Na therapeutic massage, moxibustion, cupping, and gua sha, alongside auricular, scalp, Korean cosmetic, and electroacupuncture techniques. Informed by Classical Chinese Medicine, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), Five-Element acupuncture, Japanese acupuncture traditions, and Korean Medicine, I approach treatment through careful observation, inquiry, and an appreciation for each person's unique constitution, circumstances, and health goals.

Sport Integrity & Anti-Doping

Clean Sport • Athlete-Centered Care • International Field Operations

My work in international sport is guided by a commitment to upholding athlete-centered care while protecting the integrity of competition. As a Doping Control Officer and International Blood Collection Officer, I have supported anti-doping operations at international sporting events, including world championship competition. My experience encompasses athlete notification, biological specimen collection, documentation, procedural compliance, and safeguarding awareness. I approach these responsibilities with professionalism, confidentiality, cultural sensitivity, and respect for each athlete's dignity, autonomy, and well-being.

Community Health, Education & Conflict Resolution

Prevention • Connection • Restorative Practice

My work in community health and education centers on connection, prevention, and helping people navigate challenges with greater understanding and access to support. Drawing upon experience in behavioral health, substance use prevention, recovery-oriented services, community outreach, and school-based conflict resolution education, I bring a relationship-centered approach to learning and engagement. My training as a Community Health Worker, recovery coach, and mediation facilitator informs my interest in restorative practices, health literacy, empathy, and constructive communication. Across these settings, I seek to create opportunities for people to feel heard, respected, and better equipped to participate in their own well-being and their communities.
