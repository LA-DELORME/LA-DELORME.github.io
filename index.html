01 / About

A Practical Wayfinder

Somewhere along the way, at the other end of a leash, I began to see the world differently.

For sixteen years, I operated a dog walking and companion animal care company, discovering that some of life's most meaningful lessons emerge through companionship, curiosity, and the simple act of paying attention.

Every dog embodies a unique personality, temperament, history, and way of experiencing the world. Some approach life with unrestrained enthusiasm. Others need patience, reassurance, consistency, or space. Each relationship offers opportunities to understand trust, communication, boundaries, and the importance of meeting individuals where they are.

There was joy in the everyday, resilience in the face of adversity, hope in new beginnings, and healing through connection.

Those experiences continue to shape how I understand people and approach my work.

My professional journey spans executive scheduling and travel coordination, fine dining and hospitality, nonprofit arts, entrepreneurship, Traditional East Asian Medicines, clinical healthcare, integrative skincare, international sport, community health, education, and conflict resolution.

While these experiences may appear unrelated, each offers a different perspective on the human experience. Across these settings, I return to familiar questions about what people need, what they value, how they relate to one another, and how their histories, environments, and circumstances shape their experiences.

Connecting these disciplines is an enduring curiosity about people, an appreciation for individuality, and a belief that how we show up for others matters just as much as the work we do.

Authenticity is central to how I move through the world. I believe in showing up as myself, with curiosity, humility, warmth, and a willingness to learn. Equally important is creating space for others to feel comfortable being themselves, without judgment or expectation.

For me, meaningful care is not defined solely by what we do, but by how we show up, how we listen, and how we hold space for others to feel seen, respected, and free to be themselves.

My approach draws upon careful observation, contextual understanding, reflective practice, and practical judgment. I value technical competence alongside compassion, recognizing that meaningful relationships are built through trust, consistency, mutual respect, and genuine human connection.

Although I no longer provide direct companion animal care, I remain available for consultations informed by those years of experience. The lessons I continue to draw from working with animals also inform my approach to human care, particularly the importance of trust, attentiveness, individuality, and meeting others where they are.

I remain a student of the human experience, interested in what connects us, respectful of what distinguishes us, and curious about what I have yet to discover.

To navigate with intelligence. To care with intention. To serve with gratitude.
Excellence is a standard, not a status.

Acupuncture

As a Maryland licensed acupuncturist, my practice incorporates acupuncture, Tui Na therapeutic massage, moxibustion, cupping, and gua sha, alongside specialized approaches including auricular, scalp, Korean cosmetic, and electroacupuncture. My integrative skincare work also includes microneedling. My clinical approach is informed by Classical Chinese Medicine, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), Japanese acupuncture, Five-Element acupuncture, and Korean Medicine. I draw upon these distinct traditions to provide individualized, attentive care that respects each person’s needs, goals, and overall well-being.
